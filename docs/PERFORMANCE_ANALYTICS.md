# Performance Analytics

Analytics compute profit, loss, expectancy, drawdown, Sharpe, Sortino, Calmar, ulcer index, streaks, volatility, risk of ruin, SQN, and equity curves.

Enterprise reporting exports PDF, Excel, CSV, and JSON snapshots with executive summaries, trade logs, metrics, charts, optimization results, risk analysis, and recommendations.

## Phase 12 Portfolio Analytics

Portfolio analytics extend trade analytics with NAV, ROI, ROE, drawdown, volatility, VaR, expected shortfall, attribution-ready returns, benchmark-relative alpha, beta, tracking error, and risk-score calculations across portfolios, accounts, brokers, strategies, and symbols.
