How verification works

  1. Gmail: select the Google account in Google's consent screen, approve the requested AlgoBot notification permission, then return to AlgoBot. Google verifies account ownership.
  2. Telegram: AlgoBot creates a short-lived one-time connection token. Open the AlgoBot Telegram bot, press Start, and the bot binds that Telegram chat to your AlgoBot user.
  3. Delivery: only channels with Verified status are eligible for external notifications. You can disconnect either channel at any time.

Connection credentials are stored server-side in encrypted form and are never rendered into the notification page.
