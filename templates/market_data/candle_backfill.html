BROKER DATA CONTRACT

Deriv is the OHLC source of truth

Minute-and-higher candles are persisted from Deriv native candle history. Sub-minute and tick research series are built only from persisted Deriv ticks. No synthetic market prices are generated.

WORKER TELEMETRY

Database-backed lifecycle

Worker acceptance, start time, heartbeat, symbol progress, timeframe results and terminal state are persisted in Django. Celery PENDING cannot overwrite confirmed application lifecycle evidence.
