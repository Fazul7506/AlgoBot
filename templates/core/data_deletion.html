Data Deletion Request

Use this page to request deletion of personal information or connected notification credentials associated with an AlgoBot account.

What can be deleted

Subject to verification and applicable retention obligations, a deletion request may cover your AlgoBot profile information, connected Gmail or Telegram notification credentials, notification preferences and other personal information that is not required for a legal, security or audit purpose.

Google data

If you connected Gmail, you may first disconnect the channel from Notifications. A deletion request can also ask us to remove stored Google account identifiers and OAuth credentials associated with that connection.

Records that may be retained

We may retain limited records where necessary for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, accounting, legal compliance or an audit obligation. Retained records remain protected and are deleted when the applicable retention requirement ends.

How to request deletion

Go to Contact AlgoBot and identify your request as a data deletion request. Do not include passwords, access tokens, API secrets or Google client secrets. We may need to verify account ownership before processing the request.

Processing

After verification, we will review the request against our retention obligations and remove eligible information within a reasonable period. If some information must be retained, we will explain the applicable limitation where permitted.

See the Privacy Policy for the full description of data collection, use, sharing, security and retention.
