Cookie Policy

Effective date: 29 August 2026. AlgoBot uses a limited set of cookies and browser storage mechanisms to operate the website securely and remember necessary application state.

Essential cookies

Session cookies help keep authenticated users signed in and maintain server-side session state. Django CSRF protection uses a security cookie/token to help prevent unauthorized cross-site requests.

Preferences and interface state

Where the application stores interface preferences, such as theme or navigation state, those values are used to provide the requested user experience. They are not sold or used for behavioral advertising.

Third-party authentication

When you choose a third-party authorization flow such as Google OAuth, the third party may process information under its own policies while you are on its authorization page. AlgoBot receives only the authorization response and account information permitted by the scopes you grant.

Analytics and advertising

AlgoBot does not use cookies for targeted advertising and does not sell cookie-derived information to data brokers.

Managing cookies

You can configure or block cookies through your browser. Blocking essential cookies can prevent login, secure requests and other core functionality from working correctly.

More information

For broader data practices, see our Privacy Policy. For service terms, see Terms of Service.
