About AlgoBot

Broker-connected trading operations in one workspace.

AlgoBot is software for traders and trading teams who want market intelligence, strategy research, backtesting, risk controls, execution workflows, performance attribution and operational notifications in a single workspace.

How the platform works

1. Connect. Authorize a supported broker account through the broker's secure connection flow.

2. Research. Review market data, scanners, signals, strategies, backtests and AI-assisted analysis.

3. Control. Apply account-aware risk and execution controls before orders are submitted.

4. Execute and review. Manage orders and positions, then review trade history, portfolio state and performance.

5. Notify. Optionally connect Telegram or Gmail for operational and trading notifications requested by the user.

What AlgoBot is not

AlgoBot is not a bank, broker, investment adviser or promise of financial performance. AI outputs and market signals are decision-support information and can be incorrect. Users retain responsibility for their trading decisions, broker permissions and legal obligations.

Privacy and transparency

We publish a Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Cookie Policy and Data Deletion Request page so users can review data handling before connecting services.
