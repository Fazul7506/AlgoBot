# Remote market asset policy

AlgoBot does not author, synthesize, draw, or store SVG instrument icons. The shared registry requests third-party assets from source URLs. Cryptocurrency brand marks are requested from the Simple Icons CDN and forex currency flags from FlagCDN. These are third-party identity assets, not a claim that Deriv supplies an official per-symbol SVG.

Deriv Active Symbols is the authoritative list of currently active trading symbols; its response does not include a per-instrument SVG URL. A market without a verified source asset is intentionally shown with a neutral text fallback, never a generated or family-level SVG. Adding a symbol requires verifying both its active Deriv symbol and an authentic upstream asset URL. Respect provider availability, terms, trademarks, CSP, and caching requirements.
